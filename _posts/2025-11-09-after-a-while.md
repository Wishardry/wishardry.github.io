...it's been a while. 

Life...does things. Time passes on and by, and, well, things happen.

And with the changes that come in my life...comes changes I want to make to this blog.

I can keep spreading ideas and novel information, but perhaps this blog site won't be the vibrant place I once thought it would be. And frankly, I'm ok with that, and I hope that you, my dear reader, are also fine with that. The reason is, I'm diving more into an agent project that I have, and frankly this time of life is some of the busiest and most important that I'll ever be in. There's only so much of me.
